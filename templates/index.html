Excel to Text (TXT) Converter

Excel to Text is a tool used to convert Excel spreadsheet files (.xlsx, .xls) into plain text (.txt) files. It extracts the data stored in worksheets and converts it into a readable text format. Users can upload an Excel file, select a worksheet or conversion format, and download the resulting TXT file.

How to Convert Excel to Text?

To convert your Excel documents into TXT files, follow these steps:

  1. Upload, Drag & Drop, or Select your Excel file (.xlsx or .xls) into the input box.
  2. Select the worksheet/sheet you want to convert, if the workbook contains multiple sheets.
  3. Choose the desired text format or delimiter, such as Tab,Comma, or Space .
  4. Configure formatting options if necessary.
  5. Click on the Convert to TXT button to instantly convert and download your file.

Your Excel data will be converted into a TXT file within seconds. You can then open the resulting file using Notepad, Notepad++, VS Code, or any other text editor.

FAQs

Here are some commonly asked questions about Excel to TXT converters.

How do I convert Excel to TXT?

Follow these steps:

  1. Open an Excel to TXT converter.
  2. Upload your .xlsx or .xls file.
  3. Select the worksheet you want to convert.
  4. Choose a delimiter or text format.
  5. Click Convert to TXT.
  6. Download the generated TXT file.

Can I convert XLSX to TXT?

Yes. XLSX files can be converted into plain TXT files by extracting the cell data from the spreadsheet and writing it into a text-based format.

Can I convert XLS to TXT?

Yes. Older Excel .xls files can also be converted to TXT when the converter supports the XLS format.

Can I convert a specific Excel sheet to TXT?

Yes. If your workbook contains multiple worksheets, you can select the particular sheet that you want to convert instead of converting the entire workbook.

How do I convert Excel to TXT without opening Excel?

You can use an online Excel to TXT converter. Upload the Excel file, select the required options, and download the generated TXT file without opening Microsoft Excel.

Can Excel formatting be preserved in TXT?

TXT is a plain-text format, so advanced Excel formatting such as colors, fonts, borders, formulas, charts, and cell styling cannot be preserved in the same way as an Excel file. However, the converter can preserve the actual cell values, row structure, and column arrangement using appropriate delimiters.

What is the purpose of converting Excel to TXT?

Excel files are commonly converted to TXT when spreadsheet data needs to be used by:

  • Text editors
  • Programming applications
  • Data-processing systems
  • Command-line tools
  • Legacy applications
  • Database import systems
  • Scripts and automation tools

Is there a free Excel to TXT converter?

Yes. An Excel to TXT converter can be provided as a completely free tool without requiring users to install Microsoft Excel or create an account.
